wakkt

A sticky note that shows up the moment you open
the app you want to use less.

Free · Open source (GPL-3.0) · Android · marouaneMJH
The problem

We open apps on autopilot.

  • Thumb goes to Instagram before the brain decides.
  • Screen-time reports arrive after the damage is done.
  • App blockers are all-or-nothing, so people turn them off.
The idea

Interrupt the habit at the exact moment.

Do you really need this right now?

Your own words, on top of the app, the second it opens. No lecture, no lockout, just a pause you wrote for yourself.

How it works

Three steps, fifteen seconds.

1. Pick an app

Tap “Add note”, search, tap.

2. Write the note

Good defaults. Nothing else is required.

3. Open the app

The note appears on top, instantly.

One choice

How hard should it push?

Quiet

Silent note, back in 1 h.

Gentle

Chime, back in 15 min. Default.

Firm

Covers 80%, beep, “Continue to app”.

Brutal

Sticky, full cover, alarm, repeats.

Every detail stays adjustable under “Customize”.

Impossible to ignore

Designed so you never get used to it.

  • Random visuals every opening: clashing colors, crawling patterns, glitches, TV static.
  • Hide the app behind the note at 50%, 80% or completely.
  • Sounds and voice: from a chime to a siren; the note read aloud; vibration; repeat.
  • Safe by design: no strobing, capped at 2.5 changes per second (tested).
Privacy

Private by design, not by promise.

  • No internet permission: the app cannot send data anywhere.
  • No account, ads, analytics or trackers.
  • Data stays in private storage, excluded from cloud backups.
  • Open source, so anyone can verify it.
Under the hood

Modern, small, native.

App

Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Material 3 · Hilt · Room · DataStore · Coroutines/Flow

Platform

Usage access to detect the app · overlay window to draw the note · foreground service

Media

Sounds synthesized in code · visuals generated from a random seed

Quality

Unit-tested rules and safety caps · R8-shrunk release · Android 8 to 16
