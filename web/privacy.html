Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Applies to the wakkt Android app (ma.wakkt.app) and this website.

In short: wakkt collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. Your notes, images and settings stay on your phone.

1. Who we are

wakkt is a free, open-source app maintained by marouaneMJH ("we"). The source code is public at github.com/marouaneMJH/wakkt, so every statement on this page can be checked.

2. What the app accesses, and why

3. What we collect

Nothing. wakkt does not request the internet permission. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and has no accounts. We never receive your notes, images, app list or usage.

4. Where your data lives

Everything is stored in wakkt's private app storage on your device. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it never reaches Google or another phone through wakkt.

5. Third parties

  • Text-to-speech: if you turn on "Read the note aloud", the note's text is handed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example from Google or Samsung), which processes it under its own privacy policy. Leave this option off if you don't want that.
  • Google Play: if you install wakkt from Google Play, Google processes data about the installation under the Google Privacy Policy. We only see aggregated statistics provided by the Play Console.

6. This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or external fonts or scripts. Your light/dark theme choice is saved only in your browser. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Deleting your data

Delete a note inside the app, or uninstall wakkt to remove all of its data at once. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Children

wakkt is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Changes are also visible in the project's public history.

10. Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/marouaneMJH/wakkt/issues.
