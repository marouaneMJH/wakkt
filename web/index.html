Three steps, fifteen seconds

wakkt is built so a useful note takes almost no setup. The defaults are good; everything else is optional.

1

Pick an app

Tap Add note and choose the app you open too often, from social media to food delivery.

2

Write your note

"10 minutes max." "Check your budget first." Whatever you need to read at that moment.

3

Open the app

Your note appears on top, instantly. Close it, wait it out, or let it stay, depending on how hard you want it to push.

How hard should it push?

One choice sets everything: behavior, cover, sound and repetition. Fine-tune any detail under Customize.

Quiet

A silent nudge

  • Small closable note
  • Back after 1 hour
  • No sound
Gentle · default

A friendly reminder

  • Small closable note
  • Back after 15 min
  • Soft chime
Firm

Stop and think

  • Covers 80% of the app
  • "Continue to app" button
  • Beep and double vibration
Brutal

No way around it

  • Sticky, full cover
  • Chaos sound at alarm volume
  • Repeats every 10 s

Impossible to ignore, if you want it to be

wakkt can be a calm reminder or a deliberately uncomfortable wall. You decide, per app.

Three behaviors

Sticky for the whole visit, closable for a period (5 min to 7 days, or until tomorrow), or closable forever.

Hide the app behind

Blur and cover the app at 50%, 80% or completely, with an optional "Continue to app" escape hatch.

Restless random visuals

Every opening rolls a new scene of clashing colors, crawling stripes, rings, glitches and TV static, so you never get used to it.

Sounds and voice

From a soft chime to a full-volume siren. Optional loud mode, vibration, and your note read aloud.

Your own image

Add a photo to the note: your savings goal, your kids, the gym. Whatever reminds you why.

Smart repetition

Alerts can repeat every 10 s, 30 s or 1 min while the app stays open, and stop the moment you leave.

Private by design

  • No internet permission. wakkt technically cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs.
  • Notes, images and settings stay in private storage and are excluded from cloud backups.
  • Open source: anyone can verify these claims. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Why does wakkt need "Usage access"?

To know which app is on screen, so the right note appears at the right moment. wakkt only checks the current app; it doesn't record or analyze your usage history.

Why "Display over other apps"?

That's how the note is drawn on top of the app you open. Without it, wakkt can't show anything.

Does it drain my battery?

wakkt checks the foreground app twice a second while the screen is on and pauses when it's off. Animations only run while a note is visible. If your phone kills background apps aggressively, exclude wakkt from battery optimization (Settings in the app explains how).

Are the flashing visuals safe?

The visuals are designed to be unpleasant, not dangerous: nothing strobes, colors drift smoothly, and every pattern changes at most 2.5 times per second, below the 3-per-second limit in seizure-safety guidelines. If any visual makes you uncomfortable, choose the Quiet or Gentle intensity.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. iOS doesn't allow apps to draw over other apps or detect which app is open, so wakkt can't work there.

Is it really free?

Yes. wakkt is free software under the GNU GPL v3. No in-app purchases, no ads, no premium tier.
