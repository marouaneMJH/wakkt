# wakkt – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

wakkt shows your own notes on top of apps you choose. It is built to work entirely on your device.

## What wakkt accesses, and why
- **Usage access (app usage data):** wakkt reads which app is currently in the foreground, only so it can show the note you wrote for that app. It does not record, store or analyze your app usage history.
- **Installed apps (launchable apps only):** used to show the list of apps you can add a note to.
- **Display over other apps:** used to draw your note on screen.
- **Images you pick:** if you add an image to a note, wakkt keeps a private copy on your device for that note. It is deleted when you remove the image or the note.
- **Text-to-speech:** if you turn on "Read the note aloud", the note text is passed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, which may be provided by another company (for example Google or Samsung) under its own privacy policy.

## What wakkt collects
Nothing. wakkt does not have internet permission, so it cannot send any data anywhere. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Notes, images and settings are stored only in wakkt's private storage on your device and are excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.

## Deleting your data
Delete a note in the app, or uninstall wakkt to remove all of its data.

## Children
wakkt is not directed at children under 13.
