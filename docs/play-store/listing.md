# Play Store listing – wakkt

**App name (30 max):** wakkt – notes on your apps
**Category:** Productivity · **Contains ads:** No · **In-app purchases:** No

## Short description (80 max)
A sticky note that pops up the moment you open an app you want to use less.

## Full description (4000 max)
Pick an app. Write a note. The next time you open that app, wakkt puts your note right on top of it.

"Do you really need this right now?" on Instagram. "Check your budget first" on a food delivery app. "Never share your code" on your banking app. A reminder at the exact moment it matters.

**Choose how each note behaves**
• Sticky – stays on screen for as long as you use the app
• Closable for a period – close it and it comes back after 5 min, 10 min, 1 hour, until tomorrow, or any time you choose
• Closable forever – a one-time tip

**Make it impossible to ignore**
• Hide the app behind the note: 50%, 80% or fully covered
• Random, restless visuals that change every time you open the app
• Motion, TV static and glitch effects
• Sounds, from a soft chime to a full alarm, with optional loud mode
• Have your phone read the note out loud, plus vibration
• Repeat the alert while the app stays open
• Add your own image to the note

**Private by design**
wakkt has no internet access. Your notes, images and settings never leave your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

**Permissions, explained**
• Display over other apps – to draw your note on top of the app you open
• Usage access – to know which app is on screen, so the right note appears
• Notifications – the "wakkt is active" notification that keeps the note service running
